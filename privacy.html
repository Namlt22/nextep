NextEp – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

What is stored

NextEp stores only the settings you create: whether it is turned on, intro/outro seconds, the “Next episode” or fullscreen button you picked for a site (as a CSS selector), and the name of the video server you last chose on each site (for example “Vietsub 1080 V2”). This data is keyed by the site’s domain.

It is saved with chrome.storage in your browser. If browser sync is turned on, your browser may sync some settings through your Google or Microsoft account.

What is NOT collected

NextEp does not collect your browsing history, does not read page content beyond the video player’s controls, and uses no cookies, accounts, analytics or ads. The extension makes no network requests to the developer or to any third party.

Deleting your data

Open NextEp Settings → Backup & data → “Delete all”, or remove the extension. All data is deleted.
